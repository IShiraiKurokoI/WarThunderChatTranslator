> Add, test, select, edit, or remove a custom AI translation provider.

![AI model management](images/03-ai-models.jpg)

## Add your AI translation provider

1. Open **AI Models** in the left navigation.
2. Select **Add Model**.
3. Choose the provider type following your AI service's instructions. Enter a **display name, service address, access key (if required), and model name**.
4. Optionally add a **custom prompt** to guide translation style. You can leave other advanced options at their defaults when starting out.
5. Select **Save** to return to the model list.

Your AI provider should supply the connection information. If you do not know what to enter, consult that provider's documentation rather than guessing.

## Test and start using a model

After adding a model, complete both steps:

1. Select **Test** in its row and confirm that it returns a translation.
2. Select **Use**, then go to **Translation Settings** and choose **AI Translation (Custom)**.

**Adding a model does not automatically switch chat translation to AI.** The model must be selected, and the translation provider must be set to AI.

## Manage saved models

| Action | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Use** | Switch between configured models |
| **Test** | Check whether a model can still translate |
| **Edit** | Change the service details, model, or translation preferences |
| **Delete** | Remove a model you no longer need; check the confirmation carefully |

## AI translation is not working

Make sure the model passes **Test**, is marked with **Use**, and that **Translation Settings** selects the AI provider. Then verify your AI account, available usage, and network or proxy preferences.

> 🔐 Never share your access key in public screenshots or support requests. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and usage charges.
