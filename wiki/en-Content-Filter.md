# Content Filtering

[⌂ Back to English guide](en-Home) · [Previous: Chat Voice Playback](en-Chat-TTS) · [Next: Runtime & Network](en-Preferences)

> Control inappropriate content independently for on-screen text and voice playback.

---

## Separate display and TTS switches

- **Filter on-screen text** masks matching terms as `****` in the Dashboard and Overlay.
- **Filter voice playback** affects TTS only and does not change the text shown on screen.

This is useful for streaming: a private Dashboard can keep the complete translation while spoken audio sent to the stream is filtered separately.

## Filter level and custom lists

Choose broad, normal, or severe filtering. You can add custom blocked terms and allowlisted terms, and import or export your lists. The allowlist takes priority and can be used to avoid false positives on game terminology.

The bundled Chinese and English base lists are derived from LDNOOBW; the project source is linked from the About page.

## TTS handling

For a TTS match, choose whether to remove the matched term and continue speaking or skip the entire sentence. Display and TTS behavior remain independent.
